I am a data scientist with an MSc from ITU Copenhagen and a career-long habit of finding what does not add up. My recent work has focused on the faithfulness of LLM reasoning, specifically investigating whether DeepSeek R1's chain-of-thought reflects its internal process or functions as a post-hoc rationalisation. This involved running models on university HPC systems via Docker and Singularity and applying one-proportion z-tests with Bonferroni correction to validate the results.

I spent over two years as a Teaching Assistant for the BSc and MSc Data Science programmes, where I explained concepts like Bayesian reasoning and Simpson's paradox to more than 250 students. This experience in technical communication is complemented by nearly a decade managing cash office operations in retail, where I led the regional rollout of a new back-office system and managed the gap between senior stakeholder expectations and operational reality.

Beyond my formal studies, I build tools to solve practical problems, including a local RAG pipeline for proprietary documents and agentic coding workflows using LiteLLM and Ollama. I prefer messy problems that require examining trade-offs and asking why a model is making a specific decision, rather than simply accepting the output.
